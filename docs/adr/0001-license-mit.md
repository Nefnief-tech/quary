# ADR-0001: MIT License

Date: 2026-10-04
Status: Accepted (owner decision)

## Context

Quarry is positioned as a fully open-source, community-friendly alternative to Pterodactyl with no paywalled features. The license choice trades ecosystem adoption against protection of the commons. Pterodactyl itself is MIT, which lowers friction for potential contributors arriving from that ecosystem.

## Decision

MIT.

## Consequences

- Maximum adoption and contribution freedom; companies can embed Quarry without legal friction.
- Closed-source forks are permitted; the project competes on quality and community, not license enforcement.
- Compatible with the permissive Go/npm ecosystem Quarry builds on (no license-compat concerns for dependencies).
